## Launch thread

1/ Meet Puppets: stand-in players you control, for testing anything multiplayer on your own.

Spawn a crowd, record yourself and have them replay it, or take one over and drive it with your own keys.

Fabric, Forge and NeoForge. Minecraft 1.20.1 and 1.21.1. [link]
→ puppets-trailer-landscape.mp4

2/ Press O and you get a menu. Every button shows the command it sends, so you never have to read a manual to start.
→ poster-hero.png

3/ Record yourself walking, building, opening chests or driving. Any puppet replays it.

It records what your game sends the server, so it even replays other mods' keys (vehicle controls, gun triggers, machine buttons) without knowing those mods.

4/ Scripts are plain text, one action per line:

repeat 4
  move forward
  wait 1s
  turn right
end
@Alice say done

No programming language to learn.

5/ Puppets get on by themselves too: follow you, walk somewhere, wander, or guard a spot.
→ poster-features.png

6/ Built to sit in big packs. It runs next to Carpet and in a 300-mod pack, and 100 puppets cost about 7 ms a tick.

7/ For developers: a small addon API. Add your own actions and behaviours, or listen for puppets joining. There's an example addon to fork. [link]
