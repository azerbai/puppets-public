## YouTube

### Trailer (puppets-trailer-landscape.mp4)

**Title:** Puppets: stand-in players you control (Minecraft mod, Fabric / Forge / NeoForge)

**Description:**
Testing anything multiplayer on your own? Puppets gives you stand-in players: real players to the server, controlled by you.

- A menu (press O): spawn a crowd, make them follow you, all in a couple of clicks. Every button shows the command it sends.
- They're real players: they show in the tab list and /list, take damage, get hungry, and count for anything that counts players.
- Record yourself once, and any puppet replays it: walking, building, clicking, even other mods' keys.
- Take one over and drive it with your own keys and mouse.
- Plain-text scripts to direct a whole crowd.
- Works with other mods: here a puppet flies a Superb Warfare helicopter.
- Mob puppets: take over any mob (a horse, a villager, a cow) and work it the same way.

Compared in the video: Carpet's /player (1.4.112) and Fake Players (2.2.0), Minecraft 1.20.1, Fabric.

Minecraft 1.20.1 (Fabric, Forge) and 1.21.1 (Fabric, Forge, NeoForge).
